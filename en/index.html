Rotor: Daily Lock

Privacy · Terms · Français

Rotor is a dial puzzle: spin the rings until a spoke of matching pins lines up and unlocks. Every level is checked by a solver and shows its par.

A new Daily Lock every day, the same for everyone. Safes to crack by feel, golden rushes, star chests and dial styles to collect.

Support

A question, a problem or an idea? Write to support@cibinc.ca. We answer in English or French.

Your progress is saved on your device only: to keep it on a new device, restore an iCloud or Google backup of your old one.
