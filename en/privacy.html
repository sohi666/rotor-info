Privacy policy

Rotor · Français

Effective November 7, 2026. This policy explains what information the game Rotor: Daily Lock ("Rotor") collects and how to exercise your rights. Rotor is published by Consultation Behtoyi Inc. ("we"), La Prairie, Québec, Canada.

In short: no personal data collected

Rotor doesn't ask for an account, name, email or phone number, and doesn't send any information about you or how you play to us or anyone else. The game works fully offline.

Your progress stays on your device

Your levels, stars, coins, chests, styles and streaks are stored only on your device. They may be included in your device backup (iCloud or Google), depending on your own settings. We can't access them. Deleting the app erases them.

Ads and purchases

This version of Rotor shows no ads, contains no advertising or tracking library, and offers no in-app purchases. If a future version adds ads or purchases, this policy will be updated before that version is released to describe what those services collect and your choices.

Usage statistics and crash reports

Rotor uses no analytics or crash-reporting tools. If we add any, they will be anonymous, won't be used for ad tracking, and this policy will be updated before they're turned on.

Children

Rotor isn't directed to children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Your rights and who is responsible

For any question or request about your personal information, write to support@cibinc.ca. The person in charge of the protection of personal information is the president of Consultation Behtoyi Inc., reachable at the same address. You may also contact the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec.

Changes

We'll post any change on this page with its effective date.
